## Compatibility and recovery

Preserve current session, team, runtime authority, registration and pending
schemas. No database or on-disk migrations. Existing binaries retain their

idle/restart path. Current lifecycle, creation, cluster adoption and generation

changed generation; future member reports resolve the current stable dispatcher.
Keep Codex roots and package refs. Package recovery remains forward-only.

The first application activation is an agreed idle cutover, launched outside
managed active turns without interrupting them. Later live activation must
prove active lead/member continuity and report/queue/approval recovery.
