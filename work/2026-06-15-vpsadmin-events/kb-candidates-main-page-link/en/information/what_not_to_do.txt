====== What Not to Do on a VPS? ======

  * don’t do anything illegal on your VPS, or you will be expelled
  * we don’t like seeing Tor, exit nodes are forbidden
    * hidden services are OK
  * if your VPS becomes the target of a DDoS attack more than twice, you can be expelled
    * this primarily concerns game servers, we basically haven’t recorded problems anywhere else
  * in essence, we don’t mind game servers but we have had a number of bad experiences with them
    * special conditions apply to game servers, see the information about [[Information:VPS_Params|assigning processor cores]]


Other than that, the way you use your VPS is up to you and is not subject to any regulations.
