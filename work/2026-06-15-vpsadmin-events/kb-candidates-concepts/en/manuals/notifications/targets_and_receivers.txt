====== Targets and receivers ======

A route does not send an event directly to an address. It points to a
**receiver**, which contains one or more **targets**:

  * a **target** is a concrete delivery endpoint such as an e-mail address, telephone number, Telegram chat, or webhook URL,
  * a **receiver** is a named group of targets that can be reused by several routes.

One event passed to a receiver prepares delivery for each of its enabled
targets. The same target can be attached to several receivers, and targets
can change without rewriting routes.

===== Targets =====

Open the list at
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.targets.open">**Notifications → Targets**</vpsadmin-nav>.
It shows each target's delivery method, endpoint, enabled state, and
verification state.

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:notifications:targets.png?900|List of notification targets}}

When adding or editing a target at
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.target-form.open">**Notifications → Targets
→ add or edit target**</vpsadmin-nav>, choose one delivery method:

  * **E-mail** can use the account address or a custom address. A custom address must be verified through a link in the verification message.
  * **SMS** uses an E.164 international telephone number and requires a verification code before delivery.
  * **Telegram** is paired with a particular chat using a one-time pairing command.
  * **Webhook** sends JSON to a public HTTP(S) URL. An optional secret signs the exact body in the ''X-VpsAdmin-Signature-256'' header.

A disabled, unverified, or unpaired target receives nothing. Globally
disabling one delivery method also pauses all targets of that type.

===== Receivers =====

Create a receiver at
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.receivers.open">**Notifications →
Receivers**</vpsadmin-nav>. Give it a purpose-oriented name such as
**On-call** or **Resource-change webhook**.

After opening it at
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.receiver-form.open">**Notifications →
Receivers → edit receiver**</vpsadmin-nav>, you can change its description,
enabled state, and attached targets.

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:notifications:receiver.png?900|Receiver settings and its targets table}}

Disabling a receiver stops all delivery through it without changing its
routes. A receiver with multiple targets fans out delivery, for example to
e-mail and Telegram at the same time.

The built-in **Mute** receiver is special: it has no delivery endpoint and
suppresses the matching event. It is used by mute routes, normally with
**Continue** disabled.

Continue with [[manuals:notifications:routing|Event routing]] or choose a
practical procedure from the [[manuals:notifications|notifications overview]].
