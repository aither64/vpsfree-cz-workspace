====== Notifications ======

The vpsAdmin notification system turns events into e-mail, SMS, Telegram, or
webhook deliveries. Open it from the
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.open">main **Notifications** menu</vpsadmin-nav>.

The basic flow is **event → route → receiver → target**:

  * an **event** describes what happened,
  * a **route** selects events and decides whether routing continues,
  * a **receiver** describes who or which integration should be notified,
  * a **target** is a concrete e-mail address, telephone number, Telegram chat, or webhook endpoint.

Delivery history is not an internal audit log. An event is stored there only
when routing prepares at least one real delivery. If you need a complete
external record, create a catch-all route and deliver it to your own durable
storage, for example through a webhook.

===== Advanced options =====

<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.route-grouping-form.open">Grouping on a
route</vpsadmin-nav> collects related events into one summary; the
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.groups.open">notification-group list</vpsadmin-nav>
shows pending and historical summaries. Create a reusable schedule under
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.time-intervals.open">**Notifications → Time
intervals**</vpsadmin-nav>, then attach it as an active or mute interval in
the <vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.route-time-intervals.open">route's time
interval settings</vpsadmin-nav>.

Inspect prepared deliveries and matched routes at
<vpsadmin-nav id="notifications.event-route-matches.open">**Notifications →
Delivery history → event detail**</vpsadmin-nav>.
