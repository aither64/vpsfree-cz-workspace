    maps when LXC has already mounted proc. Accounts then receive new IDs after
    restart while persistent maps and numeric file ownership retain the old IDs.
    
    Prepare and verify the selected state binds within the upstream ordered early
    mount callback, and make legacy users depend on specialfs. Preserve configured
    sources, units and mount options, existing source metadata, root-backed layouts
    and global mount dependencies. Abort activation before allocation when required
    declared backing, preparation or selected bind checks fail. Ordinary guests and
    alternative account backends retain their behavior; explicit guest specialfs
    overrides remain authoritative.
    
    Add a pure flake check and registered old-to-new upgrade tests. The correction
    changes no map format or stateVersion and needs no coordinated host update.
    It prevents new drift; recovery of already-changed identities remains separate,
    and restarting an older generation can reintroduce the ordering defect.
