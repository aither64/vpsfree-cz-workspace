====== Information for new members ======

Welcome to vpsFree.cz. This guide explains how to log in to vpsAdmin for the
first time, check your membership and connect to your VPS.

===== 1. Log in to vpsAdmin =====

<vpsadmin-nav id="session.login.open">
Log in at [[https://vpsadmin.vpsfree.cz/|vpsadmin.vpsfree.cz]] with the username
and temporary password from your welcome email. Both are case-sensitive. You
have to change the password when you first log in.
</vpsadmin-nav>

If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder. If you received only a
payment reminder without login credentials, [[information:where_to_write|contact
support]] and include your registration number.

===== 2. Check your membership and payment =====

<vpsadmin-nav id="member.list.open">
Open **Members** in the top vpsAdmin menu.
</vpsadmin-nav>

The overview shows your member ID and when your paid membership expires:

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:getting-started:members-list.png?direct|Membership overview in vpsAdmin}}

You do not have to pay the membership fee immediately. You can try the VPS for
one week before deciding whether to continue using it.

See [[information:membership_fees|Membership fees and payments]] for the current
amount, bank accounts, payment reference and payment verification. Use your
member ID as the payment reference, not the VPS ID. If you need more time to
pay, let us know before your membership expires.

===== 3. Find your VPS =====

<vpsadmin-nav id="vps.details.open">
Open **VPS** in the top menu and click the VPS ID to open its details.
</vpsadmin-nav>

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:getting-started:vps-list.png?direct|VPS list}}

The details show the VPS status, location, resources and forms for everyday
management:

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:getting-started:vps-details.png?direct|VPS details}}

===== 4. Log in over SSH =====

The VPS details contain its addresses and a basic connection command:

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:getting-started:ssh-connection.png?direct|VPS addresses and SSH connection}}

Use a password or SSH key for your first login.

==== Password login ====

<vpsadmin-nav id="vps.details.open">
Set a new, unique ''root'' password using the form in the VPS details.
</vpsadmin-nav>

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:vps-management:set-root-password.png?direct|Setting the root password}}

The default templates permit ''root'' password login. Then use the connection
command shown in the VPS details.

==== SSH key login ====

<vpsadmin-nav id="member.public-keys.add">
Save your public key under **Edit profile → Public keys → Add public key**.
</vpsadmin-nav>

<vpsadmin-nav id="vps.details.open">
Then deploy it to the running system using the form in the VPS details.
</vpsadmin-nav>

{{:en:screenshots:vpsadmin:getting-started:deploy-public-key.png?direct|Deploying a public key to the VPS}}

The [[manuals:server:ssh|SSH guide]] explains the complete process, including
server host-key verification.

===== 5. If login does not work =====

You can restore access even if the VPS network, firewall or SSH service is
broken. Follow the [[manuals:vps:console|remote console guide]], which covers
the normal console and the start-menu **Run** environment for repairing the VPS
configuration. If you need help,
[[information:where_to_write|contact the community or support]].

===== 6. Learn the VPS basics =====

  * [[manuals:vps:management|Creating and managing a VPS]] covers starting, stopping, reinstalling and other everyday changes.
  * [[manuals:vps:datasets|Datasets]] describe VPS disks, while [[manuals:vps:backups|backups]] cover snapshots, restoration and downloads.
  * [[manuals:vps:traffic|Network traffic]] covers monthly reports and live monitoring.
  * [[manuals:vps:ip_addresses|IP addresses]] explains assigned and routed addresses, while [[manuals:vps:rdns|Reverse DNS]] explains how to configure PTR records.
