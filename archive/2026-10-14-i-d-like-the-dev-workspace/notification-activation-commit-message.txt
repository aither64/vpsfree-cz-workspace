portal: activate notification workers while tabs stay open

New and refreshed notification controls should use the current worker without
requiring other portal tabs to close. Skip waiting during installation and
claim clients on activation. Observe the update within a bounded deadline and
confirm the current document controller before checking Test-result support.
Keep stale refreshes and actions from publishing an old readiness result.

Retain open documents and drafts, subscriptions, keys and existing persistent
formats. Old loaded notification scripts need not retain earlier semantics.
No unregister, resubscribe, automatic reload or data reset is required. This
activation change does not establish the cause of a missing physical display.
