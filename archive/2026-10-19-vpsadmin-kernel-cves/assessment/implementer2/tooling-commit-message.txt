Evaluate vulnerabilities introduced by exact livepatches

A cumulative livepatch can introduce a later kernel regression before its
upstream stable boundary. Evaluate reviewed source-bound introductions before
accepting a safe release or custom build, retaining unknown results for
ambiguous activation, identity, inventory and protection ordering.

Require dossier schema 2 for introduction rules so older tools reject these
assessments instead of silently omitting the runtime introduction. Preserve
schema 1 assessments and the existing history and protection rules.
