# Kernel CVEs for approval

Approval scope: select CVEs for later unpublished vpsAdmin advisory drafts. No advisory has been drafted or published.

The latest published advisory is #56, **CVE-2026-72472**, published on 23 August 2026. Its upstream fix is in 6.12.97; it is excluded from this selection along with every other previously reported CVE. The full published baseline contains 57 distinct CVEs.

The requested range includes fixes first shipped in **6.12.95 through 6.12.113**, including both endpoints. A row fixed in 6.12.95 does not mean 6.12.95 remains vulnerable. Introduction versions differ; for example, CVE-2026-74753 affects 6.12.96 through 6.12.107 and is fixed in 6.12.108.

Selection follows serious shared-kernel impact and the existing advisories' breadth. Formal CVSS High and unscored cases can qualify when the primitive and tenant path support potential privilege escalation or isolation failure. Scores below are Linux CNA CVSS 3.1 ratings, not a new vpsAdminOS-specific assessment. Potential escalation is not a claim that an exploit has been reproduced on vpsFree.cz.

**Recommended drafting batch: 41 CVEs.** The conditional and borderline groups below are not part of that batch. The exact machine-readable set is [selection.json](research/selection.json).

## Prerequisites and evidence limits

Ordinary-process candidates use interfaces such as AF_UNIX, io_uring, Landlock, timers, keys or perf. Tenant-administrator candidates use delegated devices, mounts or namespace network capabilities. SCTP ADDIP/authentication, BIG TCP/GRO, IPVS, XFRM and other specialised paths require their stated configuration. KVM cases may require Intel nested VMX, VPID or eVMCS; Intel PT perf cases require that hardware. Exact per-CVE gates and impact confidence are recorded in the [classification](research/classification.md) and [platform evidence](research/platform-baseline.md).

For CVE-2026-99999, the Linux CNA reports demonstrated host memory disclosure and code execution in a microVM-to-TAP setup. That is an attributed result in that setup; this research did not reproduce an escape from a vpsAdmin container.

Conditional cases need their specific host-enabled module, helper, service or prepared mount. CIFS lacks user-namespace mounting support in the inspected build, so a preloaded CIFS module alone does not establish a tenant trigger. BPF availability similarly does not establish every program type or speculative-path bypass.

Archived configuration and source defaults establish plausible platform relevance. They do not prove current node kernel identity, effective sysctls, loaded modules, LSM policy, enabled VPS features or livepatch activation. The historical cumulative livepatch v6 is quarantined; this list is not a recommendation to activate it.

## Sources and coverage

The primary join covers 3,493 Linux CNA CVEs across all 19 releases and was checked against all 7,484 stable commits. Forty-eight of those CVEs are already reported in vpsAdmin. Focused manual screening covers 109 additional records. The supplemental scan accounts for all 223 Critical-rated records outside that pass (208 new and 15 previously reported), retaining unknown dispositions. Together, 317 new records received focused screening and 3,128 new records remain mechanical-only and unresolved, mainly High or unscored. These screens narrow the selection; the wider remainder has not received a complete manual security audit. No claim that every other CVE is safe is made.
