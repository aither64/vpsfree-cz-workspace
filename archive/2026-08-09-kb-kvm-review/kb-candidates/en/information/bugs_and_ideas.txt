====== Where to Report Bugs and Send in Ideas for Improvement ======

Problems with membership, VPS, NAS, etc. should be reported to our support team.
If you aren’t sure where you should direct your request, write to [[support@vpsfree.org]].

We're using [[https://github.com/vpsfreecz|GitHub]] to track bugs and issues in our [[Projects|projects]]. 

^ Area                ^ Where to report                                                  ^
| vpsFree.cz website  | https://github.com/vpsfreecz/web/issues                          |
| vpsAdmin            | https://github.com/vpsfreecz/vpsadmin/issues                     |
| vpsAdminOS          | https://github.com/vpsfreecz/vpsadminos/issues                   |
| VPS templates       | https://github.com/vpsfreecz/vpsadminos/issues                   |
| Others              | [[support@vpsfree.org]]                                          |
