It is possible to run [[https://libvirt.org/|libvirt]] on [[navody:vps:vpsadminos|vpsAdminOS]] with manually or [[navody:distribuce:NixOS|NixOS]] managed network. This requires enabling **KVM** and **TUN/TAP** features.

See https://github.com/vpsfreecz/nixos-modules#provided-modules for an example and NixOS module
for libvirt network configuration.
