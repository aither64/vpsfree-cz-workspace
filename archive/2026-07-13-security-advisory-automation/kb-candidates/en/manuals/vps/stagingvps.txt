====== Staging VPS ======
Similar to the [[manuals:vps:playgroundvps|playground VPS]], you have the option to create a VPS in the staging environment. Staging is used for the development and testing of our virtualization platform [[manuals:vps:vpsadminos|vpsAdminOS]]. In staging, you can utilize more resources, but it is a development environment, and there may be unscheduled downtimes. We test new versions of the kernel, ZFS, and other components here.

===== System Resources =====
You have access to:

  * 16 CPUs
  * 32 GB of memory
  * 320 GB of disk space

It is possible to create up to 4 VPS. Their lifespan is limited to 3 months. After discussion with support, it is possible to extend their lifespan.

===== What is the purpose of staging VPS =====

  * Anything that doesn't require stability and can handle unscheduled downtimes
  * Performing more demanding operations that require more resources
  * Build/testing environment for your applications

===== What is NOT the purpose of staging VPS =====
  * As a secondary VPS for production services
  * Running services that require stability or scheduled downtimes

===== Creating a VPS =====
In staging, it is possible to create a new VPS or clone a VPS from another environment. See the article on [[manuals:vps:playgroundvps|playground VPS]].
