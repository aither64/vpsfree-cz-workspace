    ci: preserve flake input ancestry during automated updates
    
    A scheduled nixpkgs update can replace a manually advanced revision with a
    channel tip that does not contain it. Use the pinned confctl CI guard to
    validate the full candidate lock graph before accepting the update.
    
    Keep the existing flake checks and commit subject, then validate the final
    lock against the original workflow baseline before an ordinary push. A
    rejected candidate or unavailable evidence stops publication; a moved
    remote requires generating a new update from its current baseline.
    
    Reuse confctl's Git mirrors between runs, including rejected updates. A
    restored cache never skips the ancestry checks.
