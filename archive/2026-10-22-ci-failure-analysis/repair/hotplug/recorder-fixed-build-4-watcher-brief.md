has ever been bound, admission is false, no prior failure exists, and fresh
elapsed time is below the existing5s budget. No other observation gains this
classification. Query/parse/permission errors, wrong names/paths, unexpected
states and postbinding identity loss keep their strict refusal behavior.

charge only. Pending never means process absence, successful completion or
admission. It does not authenticate a signal target. Existing stream draining,
known-birth reconciliation, daemon/host/disk/log checks and cadence continue.

The last pending observation is bounded and carried into first-failure and final
receipts; samples retain the tentative scope. Only ordinary named active scope,
nonempty invocation, canonical cgroup, actual authenticated driver/timeout chain,
hard limits and selected resources allow admission. Fresh elapsed and native
exit are checked after scope reads, after required pending reads and immediately
before FIFO release following chain revalidation. The original5s never resets.
