Ruby); explicit per-VPS limit overrides apply last. Current global mode is a
hard ceiling and the VPS stop exemption always prevents automatic stops.
Ruby can reduce the allowed action but cannot raise that ceiling or bypass the
exemption. The engine validates the final combination before deciding.

New installs seed null threshold overrides and global mode observe. Preserve
all existing nonnull global settings as legacy overrides when their intent is
unknown, with a one-time transition summary identifying what masks site policy.
Only an explicit audited operator edit clears an override. No silent reset or
new duplicate settings store. The initial Ruby rule retains base limits 5/10
and follows the configured global mode, preserving existing notify/enforce
behavior while a fresh install remains observe.

### History, explanations and policy changes

Persist the policy identity, minimal decision-relevant context and normalized
effective result/reason with existing report/action audit. Avoid full user/VPS

execution. A changed loaded policy identity suppresses old undispatched warning
or stop decisions; it does not replay incidents or promote prior decisions.

recalled by a policy edit. Confirmed-stop outcome reporting remains factual
recovery. Deploy/restart relevant processes to load a new policy; no hot-reload
service or periodic policy polling is added.
